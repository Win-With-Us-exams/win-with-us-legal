This Privacy Policy describes how Win With Us (“Platform”, “we”, “our”) processes personal data for users of the mobile application and related services. It should be read together with our in-app Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy & DPDP Privacy Agreement (same effective date).

1. Who we are

Win With Us is an Indian competitive-exam practice application (SSC, Banking/IBPS/SBI, RRB, BOB/LBO-style practice, and related study tools).
Contact for privacy and grievances: arcworks3@gmail.com

2. Who this app is for (18+)

The Platform is strictly for adults 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data of children. Accounts suspected to belong to minors will be terminated and associated data deleted in line with our Terms and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP).

4. How we use AI

AI features run when you trigger them (e.g. Generate Test, Ask AI, Challenge AI). Academic queries may be sent over encrypted HTTPS to enterprise AI infrastructure to produce educational responses. Outputs can be inaccurate; cross-check with official materials. We do not use your private credentials to train public foundation models.

5. Sharing and processors

We may use infrastructure and processors such as cloud hosting (e.g. API hosting), databases, and AI API providers, solely to operate the service. Google processes data if you use Google sign-in. Embedded YouTube content is subject to Google/YouTube terms when loaded.

6. Retention

We retain account and history data while your account is active and as needed for security and legal obligations. Inactive accounts may be archived or purged after twelve (12) consecutive months of no activity, as stated in our Terms.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including salted password hashing, HTTPS/TLS in transit, and restricted administrative access. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Your rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Subject to applicable law, you may request:

  • Access to a summary of personal data we hold
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure / withdrawal of consent (account deletion)
  • Nomination of another individual to exercise rights in case of death or incapacity

Email arcworks3@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 calendar days of a verified request. You may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if dissatisfied.
