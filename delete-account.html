Win With Us · Privacy Policy

Delete your Win With Us account

Win With Us is published on Google Play by Anitha Pappala (developer account "WIN WITH US EXAM TRICKS AND LOGICS"). You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to arcworks3@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Win With Us account".
  2. Send it from the email linked to your account if you used Google sign-in, or include your username if you signed up with a username and password.
  3. We may ask you to confirm the request so that nobody else can delete your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (username, display name, email if you used Google sign-in, and login credentials)
  • Your test history, answers, scores, accuracy and rank data
  • Your AI doubts, prompts, uploaded question images and AI answers
  • Your app preferences (exam, language, theme)

What we may keep, and for how long

We may keep limited records where the law requires it or to prevent fraud and abuse (for example, records of subscription purchases and security logs), only for as long as needed for that purpose. Google Play keeps its own records of any purchases you made through Google Play.

Inactive accounts may also be purged automatically after twelve (12) consecutive months of no activity, as described in our Privacy Policy.
